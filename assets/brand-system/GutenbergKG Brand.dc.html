A flat, reproducible mark system for GutenbergKG and every KG module. The brain stays as the flagship equity; a scalable graph-badge carries the family down to a 32 px favicon. Same skeleton, one accent and one glyph per module.

01 Why touch it
It doesn't scale down

Eight tiny iconed nodes and a hex texture turn to mud below ~96 px. There's no favicon-grade version.

It fights its own spec

The brand doc calls for flat vector, no gradients, no shadows — the current art is a shaded raster render.

Modules look identical

Nine near-identical brains differ only by hue. At a glance you can't tell DocKG from DiaryKG.

Not reproducible

A one-off AI render can't be re-cut cleanly for each new adapter. The system needs a drawable primitive.
